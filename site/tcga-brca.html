1. Before the fix: the thesis method as written

Running the thesis pipeline as written on TCGA-BRCA (RNA only, 1,043 samples, 1,500 genes) gave a warning sign: held-out AUC was 0.974, but the selection missed every known ER driver (ESR1, PGR, FOXA1, GATA3 all at selection frequency 0), with no PAM50 or Oncotype DX enrichment. The diagnosis: the SKAT-based adaptive weights were invalid for continuous data (see Methods), and the lambda rule implied a false discovery rate around 0.65.

The fixed adaptive method recovers the ER program: top genes include PGR, GFRA1, STAC2, FABP7, ELF5, all ER-associated, and all four positive controls stay selected even at the stringent 0.9 cutoff (152 genes, PAM50 p = 0.0015, Oncotype DX p = 0.0038). The thesis's standard correlation-rank weights latch onto a tightly correlated but ER-irrelevant block (S100 genes) and miss the biology. Outcome-blind weighting is the wrong prior here.

3. The stability study: S1 to S3

The adaptive models were accurate but unstable (EF-Adap Jaccard 0.229, LF-Adap 0.327). Within one fixed split the method is perfectly reproducible (Jaccard 1.00 across seeds), so the instability was genuine sampling variation. Three stabilization stages, consensus groups (S1), shrunk weights (S2), stability selection (S3), attack exactly that. Hover each bubble for the full numbers.

The late-fusion caution. LF-Adap S3 gains stability (Jaccard 0.588) but loses the positive controls in most splits. If I had optimized for stability alone, I would have picked a model that throws away the ER drivers. That is why every stage is judged on prediction, stability, and biology together.

One more calibration: simple gene baselines predict ER status nearly as well (ESR1 alone: AUC 0.943; four-gene set: 0.939, over 10 splits). The paper's claim is therefore selection quality, stability plus biology, not prediction. Framing it that way is the honest read.

4. Secondary endpoint: PAM50 Basal-like vs rest

A consistency check only, since the labels come from the same RNA. The fixed pipeline selects 164 genes, held-out AUC 0.994, Jaccard 0.73. It recovers EGFR (frequency 1.0), keratins KRT6A/KRT16, and the luminal program (SCGB2A2, PIP, TFF1) as negative predictors. KRT5/KRT14 were correctly not selected: their univariate AUCs for these calls are only 0.77 and 0.66 in this dataset. The method follows the data, not the textbook.
